# DRFT — Brand & Product Spec

## Brand

**Name**: DRFT (pronounced "drift," vowel dropped).

**Rationale**: Captures the river metaphor of constant directional flow, the release of tawakkul, and the process-not-destination philosophy. The missing vowel mirrors the core idea: you do not need everything filled in to move forward. Named using the Lexicon Branding method: short, ownable, trademarkable, evocative of feeling rather than descriptive of function, slightly incomplete so the mind completes it.

**Slogans** (in order of preference):
1. Plan once. Show up daily. Trust the rest.
2. Your life, running on intention.
3. Do your part. Let go of the rest.

## Philosophy

Process over outcome. You do not chase the destination. You commit to the current and return to it daily.

**Core loop**: Set → Act → Reflect → Redirect. Plan once with clarity, act on small steps, reflect daily, redirect freely when the environment changes. Redirection is built in, so the system never collapses when life shifts.

**Spiritual root** (private, not public-facing): Tawakkul. Trust in God after doing your part fully. The public translation of the feeling is "settled, unburdened, clear." The product releases you, it does not hype you up.

**Values**: freedom, consistency, presence over quantity.

**Causal chain the product is built on**: environment + identity → intention → attention → energy → time.

**Frameworks**: 12 Week Year, OKR, Kaizen.

## Audience

**Core users** (could not live without it):
- Ambitious people with no mentor: vision but no architecture
- People mid-transition: career shift, post-burnout, reorientation
- Deep workers and builders on multi-year projects
- Seekers with scattered energy who consume a lot but convert little
- Second-half-of-life planners wanting intentional compounding

**Would not need it**:
- Sprinters who thrive on short adrenaline bursts
- Highly externally structured people whose environment already dictates focus
- Pure creatives who resist any structure
- Crisis-mode operators surviving the week
- Outcome-obsessed achievers for whom process-focus feels like friction

## Modules

Eight named modules that form a shared internal language. Users do not "check tasks," they do their RECKN, set their LENS, and HOLD.

**WAYFIND** — life-planning onboarding. Asks the right questions in the right order: set intentions, build vision step by step, set goals for main life fields.

**HOLD** — daily anchors. A deal with yourself to track small anchors that make you significantly happier. Teaches valuing presence over quantity.

**PAGES** — reading tracker. Add any book; books linked to goals get higher priority. Run reading sessions, track pages, see projected days to finish, set a reward for finishing.

**RECKN** — Kaizen hour meeting. Single-player meeting with a prepared program. Free-writing and reckoning the past, reviewing how projects are progressing.

**GROUNDWORK** — projects and goals system. Goals treated as projects: a finite number of ordered tasks; duration in weeks; drag tasks between weeks; tasks are cards, not pages; extend-tasks become groups.

**LENS** — quarter focus selection. Which project(s) lead the 12 weeks. Everything else blurs, it does not disappear.

**UNFOLD** — extend-task and task groups. A task that opens into more tasks inside it, becoming a group.

**SENSE** — AI intelligence layer, positioned as a quiet mentor and not an autopilot. Flags whether a task is vague or should be extended; predicts time spent and explains its reasoning; hides everything not currently actual.

## V0 Scope

The viable v0 is one loop: set one 12-week goal, break it into weekly tasks, act on this week, reckon weekly. Four screens plus minimal HOLD.

**In scope:**

1. **Setup (WAYFIND-lite)**: 3-4 questions landing on one goal that matters for the next 12 weeks, plus its why. Cap at 1-3 goals.
2. **The Project (GROUNDWORK-lite)**: the goal becomes a project; tasks are cards dropped onto weeks 1-12; drag between weeks. Flat — no task-groups, no AI.
3. **This Week (LENS + show-only-actual)**: the default daily screen shows only this week's tasks; the other eleven weeks stay hidden. The deliberate hiding is the feature.
4. **Weekly Reckoning (RECKN-lite)**: see what you completed, one free-write box (what happened, what I am grateful for), then the defining decision: roll unfinished tasks forward or consciously redirect.
5. **Minimal HOLD**: 1-3 daily anchors with a checkmark. Cheap to build, and it carries the retention thesis.

**Excluded from v0**: full multi-domain life vision, PAGES, all SENSE AI, UNFOLD, drift detection, project portfolio, rewards and projections. No AI at all in v0 — the product is useful without it, AI needs real usage data to be good, and shipping it early only adds risk to the thing most worth validating: retention.
